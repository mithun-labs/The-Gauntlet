## EA & Automation Policy   (PRIMARY — Terms of Use)
Terms prohibit "such software, AI, high frequency automated trading, mass data entry, or other programs that manipulate or abuse the Customer Accounts." Independent guidance indicates commercially available pre-programmed automated EAs/AI are restricted and each trader's EA parameters must be unique / not masked (secondary — UNCONFIRMED detail). A fully-automated commercial EA is therefore at minimum **Conditional**, and likely Prohibited if it constitutes a banned automated program.

## Banned Behaviors   (PRIMARY — Terms of Use, verbatim categories)
Platform/data freezing; delayed/slow data feed; trading on delayed charts; guaranteeing limit-order compliance; high-frequency automated trading / abusive programs / AI; **hedging between accounts or in concert with others**; gap trading around significant macro reports or near market close; third-party account access / trading another's account; sharing strategies / account management; opening **grossly overleveraged** or substantially-larger-than-normal positions.
- **Martingale / grid:** the verbatim Terms emphasize overleveraging and abusive automation; independent sources report grid trading and martingale are prohibited at TFT. Take the worse reading → treat **martingale/grid as Prohibited**.
