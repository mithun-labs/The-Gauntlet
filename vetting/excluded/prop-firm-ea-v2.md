## One-line mechanism
A commercial, closed-source EA (propfirm-ea.com, since 2020) offering two mode families: a **Conservative**
mode ("Single position, No hedging, No martingale, No scalping") **and an Aggressive mode that uses a

reported blow-ups) cannot be confirmed to be the non-grid Conservative one, it is excluded.

instant blow-ups is **unknown** — and instant blow-ups are consistent with the grid/Aggressive mode →
benefit of the doubt withheld → **Excluded**. (Same treatment as Forex Fury and Forex Flex.)
